1 · From your Instagram data export recommended

In the Instagram app: Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Request JSON format (not HTML) and include Posts. Instagram emails you an archive, usually within a few hours.

Works with any account type, entirely offline, and nothing expires. Captions come across as notes and each post is marked posted, newest first, below whatever you're still planning.

2 · Live sync via the Instagram API

3 · From a screenshot of your profile

Screenshot your profile grid, drop it in, and the app slices it back into tiles. No account type, no app, no waiting — the fastest way to get your current feed in.

The catch: you get screenshot-resolution thumbnails with Instagram's 3:4 crop already baked in, and no captions or dates. Fine for judging how the grid reads; not your originals.

Order comes back strictly newest-first, which is how your grid reads — unless you have pinned posts. Instagram floats those to the top of your profile but doesn't mark them as pinned in any API or export, so they'll import in date order. Drag them up afterwards if so.
